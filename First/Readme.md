# NEAT and Neuroevolution Resources

A curated collection of useful resources related to NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies), WANNs, and other neuroevolution techniques.

## 📚 Foundational Papers & Articles
- [Evolving Neural Networks through Augmenting Topologies (2002) - Kenneth O. Stanley](https://nn.cs.utexas.edu/?stanley:ec02)  
  The original NEAT paper introducing the core ideas behind topology evolution.
- [Wikipedia: Neuroevolution of Augmenting Topologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroevolution_of_augmenting_topologies)  
  A general overview of NEAT and its extensions.

## 🧠 Implementations & Codebases
- [Google Brain Tokyo Workshop: prettyNEAT (WANNRelease)](https://github.com/google/brain-tokyo-workshop/tree/master/WANNRelease/prettyNEAT)  
  A minimal WANN (Weight Agnostic Neural Networks) and NEAT implementation by Google Brain Tokyo.
- [CodeReclaimers: neat-python](https://github.com/CodeReclaimers/neat-python)  
  A well-documented Python implementation of the NEAT algorithm.

## 🌐 Other Resources
- [@miz_oka on Twitter](https://x.com/miz_oka)  
  Posts and threads related to neuroevolution and open-ended learning.
- [Open-Ended Codebook – O'Reilly Japan](https://oreilly-japan.github.io/OpenEndedCodebook/app1/)  
  Interactive app showcasing examples of open-ended learning.
